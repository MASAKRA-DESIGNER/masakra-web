## Brief
L'Oréal invited influencers from Slovakia, Czechia and Hungary to the Grand Hotel Kempinski in Portorož, 9–11 September 2024. Seven brands, one trip — a boat, a beach club, an olive-farm dinner and a party. The event needed an identity of its own: strong enough to hold seven brand logos without becoming an eighth.

## Idea
**POV — Point of View.** Every guest experiences the trip through their own lens and shares it. A heavy serif wordmark, a sunset gradient from orange to pink taken from the Adriatic evening, and a neon night version for the invitation and the party.

## Work
- Save the date, invitations in three languages, event passes, door hangers
- Check-in, photo walls, beach gates, make-up and hair rooms, mirrors, product walls
- Menus for the beach bar and the olive-farm dinner, the Glambot frame, microsite visuals
- Towels, cans, pins, beach balls, inflatable rings, bean bags and pillows
- Games — Dobble, Jenga, giant tic-tac-toe, a shooting range
