## Brief
Garnier's Collective for the Planet brings influencers out of the studio and into nature. In August 2026 two editions took place at the Boutique Hotel Pri Mlyne in Lozorno — one for Slovakia with Nadácia Ekopolis, one for Czechia with Ukliďme Česko. Guests planted and mulched trees, cleared the meadow and built benches, then sat down to a long dinner under the trees.

## Idea
One simple device: a green pinstripe frame around a white card. It reads as fresh and natural, works at A5 and at one and a half metres, and carries Garnier's global Collective for the Planet mark without competing with it. Language and partner change; the system stays.

## Work
- Invitations in Slovak and Czech, A5 programme leaflet
- Photo board „Môj sľub pre prírodu“, round boards, plinths and cubes in five sizes
- Tees, drawstring bags, bottles, cap, phone pouches, lunchbox
- Table cards and wayfinding for the dinner
