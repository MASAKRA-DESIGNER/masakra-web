## Context
Infinity & One Flow Records is the label and authorial brand of Rytmus. It stands on continuous evolution, personal expression and full creative control.

## Idea
Two ideas in one mark: **infinity** — constant growth — and **one** — a single, unmistakable signature.

## System
Minimal, timeless typography built to outlast trends. One identity across releases, cover art, merch, digital platforms and print.
